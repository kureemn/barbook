# Prompts: color inside the app

## Where it came from

After the landing page got its color, I wrote:

> The entry to the site looks great tgen you enter and doesnt feel as colorful

The agent mocked up full-width color bands on the main pages. Before deciding, I asked:

> what are your thoguhts from a UX/UI perspective

It recommended cutting back its own mockup, keeping color where it tells you something, and I said:

> Yes, build it

## What the agent did

Workouts, Program, Dashboard, Starred, and Templates now open on a full-width peach band, like the landing page. On Workouts, the band holds today's session and both start buttons. On Dashboard, it holds a very large total. Lift cards take the color of their line on the chart, and finished sessions on the Program table turn soft green.

## Choices it made

- **No red band on the Dashboard.** The mockup had one, but red should mean "tap this," and a full red band edged back toward the poster look I disliked.
- **No lift colors in History.** Real training days often mix lifts, so a color for "the main lift" would sometimes mislead.
- **Peach on every page.** A different color per page would mostly be decoration, so the mockup's blue Program band became peach.
- **The active workout screen stays plain.** It's read between sets, so it should be easy to scan.
- **Charts stay on plain backgrounds,** so the lines stay easy to read.
- **Workout details and the forms have no band,** to keep this change small.
