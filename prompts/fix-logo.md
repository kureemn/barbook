# Prompts: fix the logo

## Where it came from

A tester outside the class noticed this first, while using the live app:

> on the top left, the logo is wrong because you have the lighter weights on the inside and the heavier ones on the outside. Nobody does that.

I first listed it as something I wouldn't fix, because it didn't slow him down. Then I decided it was worth doing:

> CAN WE FIX THE LOGO

## What the agent did

It swapped the plates in the barbell drawing, so the big plates sit closest to the middle of the bar and the small ones are outside them. The same drawing appears in the top bar, on the home page, and in exercise details, so one change fixed all three.
