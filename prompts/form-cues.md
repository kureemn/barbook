# Prompts: form cues

## Where it came from

After the form videos and the redesign were in, I wrote:

> Can we do cues for the workouts themselves, I feel that's something I'm always looking up because I want to be safe/not sure if I'm doing it right

The agent mocked it up with squat as the example, and I said:

> Yes, build it

## What the agent did

Each of the 34 starter exercises now has Setup, Brace, and Move cues and a Stay safe line, in a closed **Form cues** section under its video. Exercises I add myself show no cues. A test checks that every starter exercise has complete, short cues.

## Where the words come from

The agent read StrengthLog's guide for each exercise and wrote the technique cues in its own words, with a link to the guide. Pause Bench uses the Bench guide, since it had no page of its own. Cable Row is based on the close-grip seated row, and Lat Pulldown and Tricep Pushdown are based on one variation each.

The safety notes are the agent's general knowledge, not StrengthLog's, and the app says so. Some setup and brace details, like staying tall on isolation lifts, also go beyond what the guides say.

## Choices it made

- **Closed by default,** so the sets stay close to the top of the screen. It still moves them down by about one row.
- **Three cues and one safety line,** since that's about what you can hold in your head during a set.
- **General enough for different styles,** like high-bar and low-bar squats.
- **Cues are written into the app,** not stored in the database, like the form videos. Any exercise named the same gets the same cues.
