# Prompts: less plain

## Where it came from

Once the sleeker look was live, I wrote:

> Something about the page looks very plain?

The agent agreed it had removed the loud parts without adding much in their place. It mocked up more color, a big barbell, and form video pictures. That mockup had the plates in the wrong order, so I answered:

> also remember the heavier weights go in the inside not outside but yes looks better

## What the agent did

The landing page now opens on a peach panel with a big loaded barbell, and its boxes use soft blue, green, and peach. Today's plan shows each exercise's form video picture, which you can tap to play: tiles to swipe on a phone, and a list on a larger screen. The small stat boxes use the same soft colors, and they now show on a phone's Workouts page too.

## Choices it made

- **The heaviest plates sit closest to the middle.** The app's drawing already did this. Only the mockup was wrong, and the agent saved the rule for later work.
- **Pictures don't contact YouTube until tapped.** Thumbnails load from YouTube's image server, and the video itself only loads on a tap, like the form videos in a workout.
- **The exercise list on the phone's main card is gone.** The new tiles show the same exercises, so the card only needs the session count.
- **Stats on a phone are a small change to how the page works.** They used to be on the Dashboard only. They push History further down, which I can reverse if it feels long.
