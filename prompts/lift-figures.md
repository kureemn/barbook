# Prompts: lift figures

## Where it came from

The Workouts page said "Squat" above a plain barbell, so I wrote:

> why is the squat logo look like that, maybe look like someone squatting

The agent assumed I meant the Squat rack picture in the exercise details and mocked up a new one. I corrected it:

> on the workout page

So it mocked up a figure doing the day's lift in the band instead, and I said:

> Yes, build them

## What the agent did

A squat, bench, or deadlift session now shows a simple figure doing that lift. The figure comes from the session's first exercise, and any other session keeps the plain barbell.

## Choices it made

- **Plates still show progress.** One goes on for each finished week, with the heaviest closest to the middle.
- **Bench plates are rings.** The bench figure is seen from the side, so the plates stack up as circles.
- **The landing page and "Ready to lift?" keep the barbell,** since there's no session to match.
- **The Squat rack picture didn't change,** since that wasn't the one I meant.
