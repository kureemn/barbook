# Prompts: sleeker look

## Where it came from

After the tester's fixes were merged, I looked at the whole app and wrote:

> Aeshetic of the website feels not pleasign, thinking of websites like Airbnb ro Apple that are kinda sleek, but obviously it's a powererligting app

The agent mocked up a cleaner version. I shared a Mobbin page for inspiration, but Mobbin blocked the agent's browser, so it never saw those screens. I named two apps instead:

> Headspace, Appkle mUSIC, THINK THOSE

It worked from what it remembered of them and mocked up the Workouts and Dashboard pages. I then asked:

> What woukld the full dashboard look like

That mockup left out the body diagram, and the agent had said everything was still there. I caught it:

> where did the images go

It put the pictures back in the plan, and I said:

> Yes, build it

## What the agent did

It replaced the tall all-caps headings with one font in sentence case, and the cream page with white and soft tints. Red is now saved for the main button and the big total. The main card is a soft peach, buttons are pills, and most boxes lost their outlines.

The dashboard now leads with the total, which also holds the bodyweight comparison. Every section and picture stayed. Pages are narrower on large screens, and dark mode got the same treatment.

## Choices it made

- **Start my own workout stayed a real button.** Its first mockup made it a small link, which would have brought back the tester's problem.
- **A deeper red than Apple Music's.** White text on it needs to pass the 4.5:1 contrast minimum, and this red is about 5:1.
- **The lift colors on the charts didn't change.** It reran the color check against the new backgrounds, and they passed in light and dark mode.
- **The chart keeps its legend.** The mockup put lift names beside the lines, but those can overlap when two lifts are close.
- **The words stayed the same, apart from the dashboard's total card.** History rows still list exercises and muscles, even though the mockup was shorter, since this was only meant to change the look.
